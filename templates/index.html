    Preprocessing Rationale & Dropped Predictors

    Following empirical statistical analysis in customer_churn.py:

    • Gender & PhoneService Dropped: Chi-Square ($\chi^2$) tests proved gender ($p > 0.05$, Cramér's V $\approx 0.008$) and PhoneService show no significant statistical relationship with churn. Dropping them prevents overfitting.
    • Multicollinearity Removal: Duplicate flags such as OnlineSecurity_No internet service were removed to eliminate exact collinearity with InternetService_No.
    • Numerical Normalization: tenure and MonthlyCharges are scaled via StandardScaler. The right-skewed TotalCharges variable is normalized via the Yeo-Johnson PowerTransformer.
